# Clothing

Which verb goes with which garment. Small topic, but a persistent one — first
flagged in class 25 Jul 2026, re-asked from scratch on 2026-08-01, so it has
survived one full round of "learning" without sticking.

Status labels: `new` · `shaky` · `solid` · `mastered` — see `MAP.md`.

## Language

### Core (N5)

#### 着ます vs はきます — the waist line — `shaky`

**The rule is the waist line.**

| Verb | Body area | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 着ます（きます） | above the waist | シャツ、ジャケット、コート、ワンピース |
| はきます（履きます） | below the waist | ズボン、スカート、くつ、くつした |

Hook: はきます = things you **step into / pull up**; 着ます = things you put your
**arms or head through**. 履 carries the footwear sense.

**Edge case:** a dress (ワンピース) takes 着ます despite covering the legs — it
goes on over the head, so it's an upper-body action.

#### The rest of the set — `shaky`

| Verb | Used for |
|---|---|
| **かぶります**（被ります） | head — ぼうし |
| **かけます** | glasses — めがねを**かけ**ます |
| **します** | accessories — ネクタイ、ネックレス、マフラー |
| **つけます** | attach — ヘアクリップ |

**Correction to the 25 Jul class notes:** they record *"spectacles: かくます"* —
かくます is not a word. It is **かけます**. Likely misheard in class.

The class notes' own fallback strategy, recorded verbatim: *"for too many and i
don't know: きますとはきます / everything else: します (but it's pretty broken)"* —
accurate self-assessment; します is a genuine catch-all for accessories but does
not cover garments.

#### 着ます and 来ます are homophones — `new`

Likely the real source of the confusion, and worth separating out:

| Kanji | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 着ます | きます | wear (upper body) |
| 来ます | きます | come |

Distinguished only by particle and context: シャツ**を**着ます (を = object) vs
学校**に**来ます (に = destination). See `reading_traps.md` — this is one of four
tracked same-reading pairs.

#### Sidestep: 試着 — `new`

試着 ("try on") contains 着 already, so it covers clothing generally without
forcing a choice of wearing verb. Full entry in `shopping.md`.
