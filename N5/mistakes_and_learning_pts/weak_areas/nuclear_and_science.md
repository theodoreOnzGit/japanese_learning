# Nuclear and Science

The user's own subject area — nuclear science, radiation, the Fukushima trip.
Not a JLPT topic, but the vocabulary they most want to *use*, and the source of

Status labels: `new` · `shaky` · `solid` · `mastered` — see `MAP.md`.

## Language

### Core (N5) — words already in active use

#### 放射線 (ほうしゃせん, "radiation") — `shaky`

**Quiz miss (2026-07-25):** asked to translate "I'm interested in radiation"
(に興味がある round); answered 核エネルギーに興味があります instead of
放射線に興味があります. The grammar was fully correct — a *different*
also-known noun was substituted for the one asked for, possibly because
核エネルギー had been more recently drilled.

Keep 放射線 and 核エネルギー in rotation as **distinct vocabulary, not
interchangeable answers.**

#### 原発 (げんぱつ, "nuclear power plant") — `new`

Everyday abbreviation of 原子力発電所 (げんしりょくはつでんしょ), used constantly
in Japanese media about Fukushima. Used on the slides with the positional noun
近く: 双葉のホテルは原発の近くです。

#### 線量計 / 調べる / 危険 / どのくらい — `new`

From the "questions I wanted to investigate" slide (2026-07-25):

- **線量計** (せんりょうけい) — "dosimeter"
- **調べる** (しらべる) — "to investigate / check." 調べたい = want to investigate.
- **危険** (きけん) — "dangerous / danger." **な-adjective** — the original attempt
  used bare English "dangerous" + です, which doesn't work; です needs an actual
  Japanese adjective or noun. Past negative: 危険じゃなかった.
- **どのくらい** — "how much / to what extent"

**Mistake caught in the same message:** 福島で放射線がりますか →
**福島に放射線がありますか**. で marks the location of an *action*, but existence
(あります) needs **に**. Also がります was missing あ.

### Stretch (N4) — precision vocabulary

#### 核 vs 原子 vs 原子力 — `new`

Asked 2026-07-25 while refining a job title.

| Term | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 原子 | げんし | the whole atom |
| 核 | かく | nucleus / core — and the general "nuclear-" root |
| 原子核 | げんしかく | atomic nucleus (原子 + 核) |
| 原子力 | げんしりょく | atomic power/energy — the power-plant/industry sense |

**核** is the common compounding root: 核兵器 (かくへいき, nuclear weapon),
核実験 (かくじっけん, nuclear test), 核戦争 (かくせんそう, nuclear war).

Confirmed: **核 alone carries no weapons implication** — it's the general
"nuclear" root. 原子力 leans power/energy industry; 核科学者 leans nuclear
physics.

#### 科学者 (かがくしゃ, "scientist") — `shaky`

**Garbled on first attempt (2026-07-25):** produced 「くがくがくしゃ」.

科学者 = 科学 ("science") + 者 (person-suffix, same as 医者/忍者/学者).

Final choice for the slides: **核科学者（かくかがくしゃ）**, switched from
原子力科学者 for familiarity with 核.

Also flagged: **セオドア** is the conventional katakana for "Theodore"
(ドア matches the English "-dore," like ドア "door"). セオドウ isn't wrong, just
non-standard.

#### Reading numbers and units aloud — `new`

- **μSv/h** — read マイクロシーベルト毎時 (まいじ) in full; in speech
  マイクロシーベルト is usually enough.
- **Latin letters spell out individually:** SQ633 = エスキュー ろく さん さん;
  AI = エーアイ. See `presenting_and_formal.md`.
- **Decimals use てん:** 0.11 → れい てん いち いち. See `numbers_time_dates.md`.

## Cultural

### Talking about Fukushima — `new`

Not yet a logged mistake, but worth holding in mind when presenting: the 2011
disaster and the evacuation zones are a live, sensitive subject in Japan,
particularly around **風評被害 (ふうひょうひがい, "reputational damage")** — the
harm done to Fukushima producers and residents by fear rather than by measured
risk.

The user's own data supports the careful version of this: readings at 双葉駅
(0.11 μSv/h) matched Changi Airport (0.10) and Narita (0.11), while the flight
there peaked at 1.20 — and one monitoring post near an abandoned building read
1.036. So "it depends where you stand" is both the honest finding and the
respectful framing.

Register advice already applied on the slides: state **measurements as fact**
(0.11 μSv/hでした), and **judgements as opinion** (危険じゃなかったと思います) —
see `presenting_and_formal.md` → 〜と思います.
