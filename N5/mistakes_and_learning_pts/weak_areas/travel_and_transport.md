## Language

### Core (N5)

#### 入る (はいる) vs 帰る (かえる) vs 戻る (もどる) — `shaky`

Flagged 2026-07-18 — the user thought はいります meant "return." It does **not**.

| Verb | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 入ります | はいります | enter / go into a place |
| 帰ります | かえります | return (home) / go back |
| 戻ります | もどります | return / go back (to a spot) |

- 入る takes に for the place entered: 部屋に入る, お風呂に入る ("take a bath"),
  大学に入る.
- **Conjugation trap:** 入る *looks* like a る-verb (ends -iru) but is actually a
  **う-verb (godan)** → 入り**ます**, 入っ**て**, 入ら**ない**. Same sneaky group
  as 帰る (かえる) and 走る (はしる).
- Contrast: 家に**帰ります** ("return home") vs 家に**入ります** ("go into the
  house") — same frame, the meaning is all in はいる vs かえる.

#### 速い (fast) vs 早い (early) — `shaky`

Flagged 2026-07-25 on 速いですね！(about the shinkansen). Same kana **はやい**,
two kanji: **速い** = fast (speed) vs **早い** = early (time). See
`reading_traps.md`.

### Stretch (N4)

#### に vs を with 乗る / 降りる — `shaky` (3 failed attempts)

Originally from a quiz mistake (screenshot:
`../particles_ni_and_so_on.png`). **The most-missed item on this list.**

乗る takes **に** (電車**に**乗る — moving *onto/into* the vehicle, に marks the
goal), but 降りる takes **を**, not に (電車**を**降りる — moving *out of* the
vehicle, を marks separation, same pattern as 家を出る). The instinct has been to
use に for both.

**Full pattern:** [station] **で** [vehicle] **に** 乗る /
[station] **で** [vehicle] **を** 降りる.

The station itself takes **で** (place where the action happens) — に/を mark the
*vehicle*, not the station. Two different nouns, two different jobs.

**Attempt history:**

1. *(quiz, screenshot)* — defaulted to に for both.
2. *(2026-07-25, Fukushima slide 4)* — 「東京駅乗りました、そして仙台駅降りました」
   — dropped the particle entirely. Corrected to
   東京駅**で**、はやぶさ**に**乗りました。そして、仙台駅**で**降りました。
3. *(2026-07-25, 仙台→双葉)* — 「仙台駅のりました、双葉駅を降りました」— dropped
   で on 仙台駅 again, and applied **を** to the *station* instead of で. Suggests
   the mental model is "降りる takes を" without yet distinguishing that を marks
   the *vehicle being exited* while で marks the *place where the exit happens* —
   easy to conflate when only the station is mentioned and no vehicle is named.

Still not automatic. Keep drilling.

#### から〜まで ("from〜to") + そして — `shaky` (1× correct unaided)

First self-produced use 2026-07-25, correct without help:

> Changi空港**から**成田空港**まで**行きました。**そして**、東京に行きました。
> "I went from Changi to Narita. Then, I went to Tokyo."

から〜まで marks a range's start/end (space or time); そして chains full
sentences in sequence. One clean use — one more clears it.
Full write-up: `N5.tex` → Grammar → から〜まで.

#### に with point-in-time, NOT with duration — `new`

Flagged 2026-07-25 — wanted に after a duration (九日間) by instinct. Wrong.

- **Point-in-time** (a specific moment: 2025年, 3時, 月曜日) takes **に**.
- **Duration/span** (九日間, 三年間) takes **no particle** — the 間 counter
  already makes it adverbial.

> ２０２５年**に**、九日間、福島**に**行きました。
> "In 2025, I went to Fukushima for nine days."

2025年に (point in time) and 福島に (destination) both take に; 九日間 (duration)
takes none. Reading link: 九日間 (ここのかかん) reuses ここのか, the irregular
9th-of-the-month reading — see `numbers_time_dates.md`.

#### そのうち + いました (not ありました) — `new`

Flagged 2026-07-25. **そのうち** ("of that / among those") narrows a total down
to a subset: 9 days in Japan total, そのうち 3–4 days in Fukushima.

Also reinforced: **福島にいました** (not ありました) — います is for animate
beings, あります for inanimate existence. 行きました ("went") vs いました ("was
staying"). Reading: 三、四日間 (さん、よっかかん) reuses みっか/よっか.
