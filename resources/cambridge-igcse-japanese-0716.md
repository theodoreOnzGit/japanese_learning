## Level and fit

IGCSE Japanese as a foreign language sits roughly at **CEFR A2–B1**, i.e. around
**JLPT N4**, but the papers *start* lower and climb. Paper 2 opens with a
supermarket signage matching task (くすり・くだもの・やさい・出口・トイレ・パン・
バーゲン) and a 〜がほしいです question — comfortably N5.

That makes this set usable as-is: early questions suit the user's current level,
later ones stretch toward their study partner's N4–N3.

**Overlap with tracked weak areas** (`N5/mistakes_and_learning_pts/weak_areas/`):

- Paper 2 signage task → `shopping.md` (スーパー, 〜屋, やさい), `describing_and_sentence_building.md` (〜が欲しい)
- Paper 3 role plays → `shopping.md` (試着, 〜てもいいですか), `everyday_conversation.md`
- Paper 1 listening → the one format that can partly address the pitch-accent /
  listening gap noted in `reading_traps.md`, since audio transcripts are provided
